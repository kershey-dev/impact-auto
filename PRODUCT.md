# Auto Movers
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Purpose
An interactive landing-page prototype for reviewing visual design, scroll flow, and interactions before a production build.
## Audience
Vehicle owners considering collision repairs, paying directly or seeking insurance guidance.
## Confirmed content
Auto Movers Corporation. Collision and vehicle damage repair, insurance guidance, photo-based initial repair estimates. Tagline: Your Trust, Our Passion. Three supplied before/after image collages are the evidence source.
## Constraints
No fabricated statistics, testimonials, affiliations, turnaround promises, insurance approvals, or live form submission. Preserve supplied photographs. They show different viewpoints; comparisons must not claim image alignment. Services and address need owner confirmation before public launch.
## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript for this prototype. Private Sites deployment. No persistence or external enquiry delivery.

## Approved visual anchor
The user-provided Auto Movers Road-Ready Restoration Storyboard governs the landing-page composition and interaction sequence.
